Sign-In Flow

Three steps from install to sync.

The website is the guide. The extension popup and Profile surface are the account controls.

01

Install from the Store

The permanent Web Store ID is what connects CoverCraft to its approved Google OAuth redirect.

02

Open the extension popup

Click Sign In With Google directly below the account status. No website login page is required.

03

Manage sync in Profile

Open Dashboard → Profile to sync now, sign out, inspect storage status, or manage your active identity.

Local ZIP

No account required.

Local mode remains a complete BYOK workflow for generation, profile imports, saved sessions, and exports. It intentionally does not connect to production Google sign-in or Firebase sync.
